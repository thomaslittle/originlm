# Provider brand marks

Monochrome marks used by the demo and the desktop manager to identify providers.
They are tinted with `currentColor`; brand marks are trademarks of their owners
and used only to identify the provider. This repository's MIT license does not
grant trademark rights.

## LobeHub icons (MIT)

Source: `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.1, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons,
monochrome variants. License and full attribution: see
`apps/demo/src/assets/providers/README.md` and
`apps/demo/src/assets/providers/licenses/`.

Files: anthropic, claude, claude-code, codex, gemini, grok, lmstudio, local,
ollama, openai, opencode, xai.

## Simple Icons (CC0)

`cursor.svg` is the Simple Icons Cursor glyph (CC0 1.0),
https://simpleicons.org, normalized to `fill="currentColor"`. The LobeHub set
does not include Cursor.

## Modifications

Mechanical only: `currentColor`, no fixed sizes or colours. Path data unchanged.
